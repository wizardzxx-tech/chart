# Evidence review and implemented corrections

Reviewed 13 October 2026. The website, affected figures and their PPT/PDF exports distinguish verified quotations from independently measured markets, analyst scenarios and unresolved source checks. The investment thesis is conditional on funded experiments and supplier orders.

## Implemented changes

- Figures 11–12 and the interactive funnel no longer present universal pre-AI/AI molecule counts or a synthetic cohort per clinical entrant. The page distinguishes small molecules from proteins/antibodies and uses 1× / 2× / 5× / 10× measurement-workload sensitivities. These are model inputs, not forecasts of fully profiled candidates.
- Figure 03 replaces unsupported customer spending percentages with a qualitative product-use map. Research hospitals are explicit. Food, environmental and industrial-chemical uses are outside this life-science scope; clinical IVD analyzers are not included in research-instrument examples.
- Figure 06 and the calculator separate physical quantities from spending. Price ratios, usable wells and reagent overhead are explicit scenario inputs.
- Figure 07 replaces the unsupported 100% funding allocation with hypothetical total funding × 5% / 10% / 20% tool-purchase capture. These are cumulative sensitivities without a fixed horizon, not observed capital raised, vendor orders or annual TAM.
- Figure 02 retains the quoted F&S values and correct arithmetic but labels the issuer-commissioned scope. The China/global ratio is not multinational suppliers’ China revenue exposure.
- Figure 12 retains the source-confirmed supplier/ticker map, labels recombinant-protein market disagreement and withholds the two Duoning headline market values pending source recovery.

## Workflow and clinical evidence

- Classical docking evaluated170 million compounds in2019 and synthesized/tested44 and549 for two targets. This is a study-level example, not a clinical-entrant cohort. [Lyu et al., Nature2019](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41586-019-0917-9).
- A classical docking platform screened approximately1.3 billion compounds in2020. “Pre-AI” is not a universal cutoff; the distinction is method and workflow. [Gorgulla et al., Nature2020](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8352709/).
- DSP-1181 was the350th compound synthesized in one company-reported program. [Sumitomo technical report2021, PDF4](https://www.sumitomo-chem.co.jp/english/rd/report/2021E_3.pdf).
- REC-617 optimization synthesized136 novel compounds, according to company reporting. This is not a controlled industry-wide comparison. [Recursion2024 annual report](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1601830/000160183025000064/rxrxannualreport2024.pdf).
- Insilico’s78-molecule statement is company-reported and not used as a generic funnel parameter. The78 in a prominent Nature trial description refers to participants; these units must not be confused. [Company case study](https://insilico.com/casestudy).
- Pooled protein screens and purified individual profiles are distinct. Bennett et al. screened approximately80,000 pooled designs then confirmed selected binders individually; substantial pooled protein screening also existed in2017. [Bennett et al.2023](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41467-023-38328-5), [Chevalier et al.2017](https://www.nature.com/articles/nature23912).
- BIO’s7.9% Phase I-to-approval estimate covers2011–2020. Wong et al.’s13.8% covers2000–2015 with different data/methods. The two-study span is not a confidence interval, current consensus or an AI comparison. Neither is an expected output of the removed synthetic funnel. [BIO report](https://go.bio.org/rs/490-EHZ-999/images/ClinicalDevelopmentSuccessRates2011_2020.pdf), [Wong et al.](https://doi.org/10.1093/biostatistics/kxx069).

## Unit-economics calculations

Baseline:100,000 tests ×100µL =10L; ceil(100,000 /96)=1,042 destination plates;100,000 tips at1/test.

Example:1,000,000 tests ×5µL =5L; ceil(1,000,000 /1,536)=652 destination plates;100,000 tips at0.1/test.

Plate-count ratio =652/1,042 =0.625720. Plate-spend ratio =0.625720 × comparable plate ASP ratio. Break-even ASP =1,042/652 =1.598160. At1×/2×/4× ASP, spend ratios are0.625720 /1.251440 /2.502879. These price multipliers are scenario inputs, not vendor quotes. The review’s “plastic spend probably rises” is not established without equivalent product quotes and consumption scope.

Calculator reagent volume =tests ×usable assay µL ×(1 + case-specific extra-reagent overhead)/1,000,000. The baseline uses its own overhead input. New destination plates =ceil(tests /floor(wells ×usable-well fraction)); baseline plates assume100% usable wells. Price ratios multiply quantity ratios separately for reagent, plate and tip spend. Real workflows can also require source plates, reservoirs, controls, retests and instrument capital. [Beckman’s source-container and dead-volume documentation](https://www.beckman.com/supplies/echo-qualified-supplies). Average tips/test is a model input, not a recommendation to reuse biological-sample tips.

Funding scenarios:US$50/100/200bn ×5%/10%/20% capture imply cumulative tool purchases of [2.5,5,10] / [5,10,20] / [10,20,40] US$bn. No fixed time horizon or empirical capture share is asserted. Exclude labor, clinical execution, compute/data/software, facilities and funding not spent on tools. CRO fees and embedded vendor-input purchases must not be added together. [Recursion2025 10-K](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1601830/000160183026000039/rxrx-20251231.htm), pp.147–148, illustrates diverse R&D cost categories, not universal cash shares.

An incremental equipment budget need not resemble the composition of existing annual tool sales. The reviewer’s30% equipment versus64% market-reagents comparison alone is not an arithmetic contradiction. Unsupported allocation shares were removed because their evidential basis was missing.
