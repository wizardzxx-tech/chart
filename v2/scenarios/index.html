EDIT THE TRANSMISSION MECHANISM

What must be true?

The financing examples do not establish current global Build/Test capital stocks, running costs or family elasticities. Every conversion input below is an editable analyst assumption.

Equipment, lab execution and baseline cohort

Annual lab costs include personnel and shared operating costs. Baselines describe an illustrative AI-exposed cohort, not all installed global LST capacity. Rollout realization applies once to the incremental funded capacity. With zero new operating support, pure equipment CAPEX cannot fund sustained additional experiments in this model.

Six-family sensitivity coefficients

Workflow presets reset these coefficients. The response exponent describes net input growth: 1 = proportional, below 1 = lower consumption/replacement per unit of activity, above 1 = more readouts per unit. The price factor applies to incremental purchases only.

FUNDING BACKSOLVE

What funds the target?

Total funding needed for both stages, holding every other assumption fixed. No target is guaranteed. Operating funding is assumed to continue at the modeled annual rate; a post-program funding cliff is not modeled.

Formulas and interpretation
Annual total funding
Total program funding / deployment years.
Annual wet-lab equipment / lab execution
Equipment: annual total × wet-lab share × equipment share. Lab execution: annual total × wet-lab share × (1 − equipment share).
Installed stage increment
Total equipment funding × stage share × productive commissioning fraction / baseline stage equipment stock.
Recurring-funded stage increment
Annual lab execution funding × stage share / baseline annual stage lab costs.
Realized stage throughput
1 + rollout realization × productivity × min(installed increment, recurring-funded increment). Capped at 10× for this cohort; a desired 2× floor is shown separately from actual funding support.
Family activity driver
Build share × Build throughput + Test share × Test throughput.
AI-cohort purchasing index
1 + (activity driver ^ response exponent − 1) × incremental price factor. Purification separates lab/analytical and manufacturing portions; the latter also requires an explicit growth pass-through.
Whole-family dilution
1 + hypothetical AI exposure share × (AI-cohort purchasing index − 1). This assumes unchanged non-AI purchases and is not a measured global market forecast.

CALIBRATION CONTEXT · PRIMARY ANNOUNCEMENTS

What has actually been announced?

These examples establish mechanisms, scale and time horizons. They cannot be added into a complete global infrastructure total or treated as all-new wet-lab procurement.

Why retain US$10–200bn and 2–5 years?

US$10–50bn is an exploratory global scenario range. US$100–200bn represents a transformational stress case. Five-year commitments have direct precedents; the two-year setting tests accelerated deployment. No probability is assigned to any preset.

Actual grants, pharma R&D budgets, equity funding and strategic partnerships are possible funding channels. Spend conversion still depends on procurement, experiment execution and continued operating budgets. The source examples do not establish the model’s baseline costs or elasticities.
