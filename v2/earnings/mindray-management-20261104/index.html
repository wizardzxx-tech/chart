Domestic market: Easing policy pressure and continued market share gains in IVD

Management indicated that the regulatory environment for China's IVD industry is showing signs of improvement. Recent adjustments to laboratory testing service fees across several provinces have been more favorable than previously expected, with some testing fees remaining unchanged or even increasing. Management expects the impact on Mindray's IVD ex-factory prices to remain below 10% in 2027. Following six years of volume-based procurement (VBP), which resulted in cumulative end-market price reductions of approximately 40%, management believes that IVD pricing has largely returned to reasonable levels and that the major pricing pressure from VBP is nearing an end. Meanwhile, refinements under DRG 3.0 could help stabilize testing volumes following previous declines. Mindray's domestic IVD sales volume grew by over 20% in 2026, supported by import substitution, particularly in hormone and cardiac marker testing. Management expects its market share in biochemistry, immunoassay and coagulation testing to increase from approximately 10% in 2025 to 15% in 2026 and 20% in 2027, driven by further penetration into high-end hospitals and the expansion of county-level laboratory testing centers. For domestic medical equipment, management expects channel inventory to return to a normalized level of slightly over two months by end-2026, providing a more favorable basis for growth in 2027, although potential equipment VBP remains a key uncertainty.

Overseas business: Sustained growth in Europe and stronger North American momentum

Management highlighted healthy underlying growth across overseas markets, with Russia being the primary exception in 3Q26. Delays in product registration amendments resulted in an estimated US$40mn revenue shortfall in Russia during the quarter. Management characterized the disruption as temporary, noting that ultrasound shipments have partially resumed, while patient monitoring and life support products remain affected and IVD operations have been largely unaffected. Excluding the Russia impact, management estimated that international revenue growth in 3Q26 would have exceeded 15% YoY in USD terms. Europe has maintained strong growth since 2024, supported by high market entry barriers, a relatively stable competitive landscape and increasing demand for automation and efficiency improvements in healthcare delivery. North America is expected to accelerate in 2027, supported by a newly secured long-term customer contract that could contribute approximately US$40–50mn in annual revenue, driving regional growth of 10–15%. Emerging markets, including Latin America, India and the Middle East, also maintained solid momentum, while Indonesia showed signs of recovery. Overall, management expressed confidence in achieving double-digit international revenue growth in 2027, with further expansion of IVD businesses in developed markets potentially supporting more sustainable long-term growth.

Profitability: Favorable IVD product mix offset by FX and component cost pressures

Despite continued pricing pressure, management noted that domestic IVD gross margin improved by approximately 1ppt in 2026, even as ex-factory prices declined by more than 10%. This was primarily driven by increasing penetration into high-end hospitals, which typically generate a higher proportion of reagent revenue, alongside ongoing manufacturing efficiency improvements. Management expects this favorable product mix trend to continue, particularly in overseas IVD markets, where reagents currently account for approximately 50% of revenue, compared with around 80% domestically. Nevertheless, overall profitability remains exposed to two major external headwinds. First, FX fluctuations represented the largest drag on earnings in 2026, with a shift from FX gains in the previous year to losses resulting in a negative earnings impact of over Rmb1bn. Given the company's substantial and growing USD exposure, the impact in 2027 remains highly uncertain. Second, rising semiconductor and electronic component prices have added cost pressure since 3Q26, with an estimated Rmb300mn incremental cost in 2H26 and a further Rmb300–400mn expected in 2027. Management indicated that semiconductor inflation alone could reduce the group's gross margin by approximately 1ppt in 2027, even if current component prices remain unchanged. With limited opportunities to raise selling prices in the current competitive environment, passing higher costs on to customers could remain challenging.

Outlook: Improving revenue visibility in 2027, with earnings recovery subject to external headwinds

Management expects overall revenue growth to accelerate in 2027, supported by improving domestic demand, the completion of channel destocking and continued overseas expansion. In China, medical equipment revenue is expected to return to growth as inventory levels normalize, while positive end-market procurement trends provide further support. Domestic IVD growth should continue to benefit from easing policy pressure, import substitution and county-level laboratory development. Internationally, sustained European expansion, new North American customer contracts and the normalization of Russian operations are expected to support growth. Beyond 2027, management outlined several major product launches, including a premium cardiac ultrasound system targeted for end-2026, mass spectrometry products in 2028 and the company's first multi-port surgical robot in 2029. In addition, approximately 20 new immunoassay tests are expected to be introduced over the next one to two years, potentially strengthening Mindray's competitiveness in high-end IVD. While management expressed confidence in improving revenue momentum in 2027, it remained cautious on the earnings outlook, given uncertainties surrounding RMB appreciation, semiconductor cost inflation and the extent to which favorable product mix shifts could offset these pressures.
