3Q26 results: Solid revenue growth, but earnings lagged.

United Imaging Healthcare (UIH) reported 3Q26 revenue of RMB3.53bn, up 24.1% YoY, accelerating from 17.2% growth in 1H26. However, attributable net profit increased by only 13.0% YoY to RMB138mn, reflecting continued pressure on profitability. For 9M26, revenue grew 19.4% YoY to RMB10.58bn, while attributable net profit declined 7.6% YoY to RMB1.04bn. We attribute the earnings weakness primarily to higher R&D expenses, share-based compensation and FX losses. R&D expenses increased approximately 35% YoY in 9M26, reflecting continued investment in next-generation imaging equipment, core components and AI technologies. Meanwhile, share-based compensation created an incremental YoY earnings drag of RMB146mn, while FX losses reached approximately RMB150mn, compared with just RMB1.6mn a year ago. Excluding FX impacts, attributable net profit would have increased 9.5% YoY in 9M26 and 22.5% YoY in 3Q26, suggesting a more resilient underlying operating performance.

Revenue breakdown: Domestic growth accelerated, while overseas expansion remained robust.

Based on our calculations, domestic revenue grew approximately 15% YoY in 3Q26, accelerating from 8.5% in 1H26, supported by continued market share gains and increasing penetration of high-end products. UIH gained 2.2ppt in overall domestic market share in 9M26, including 1.6ppt in MR and 7.8ppt in molecular imaging (MI). Overseas revenue grew approximately 45% YoY in 3Q26, compared with 54.5% in 1H26, with Asia-Pacific remaining the key growth driver. Service revenue increased approximately 24% YoY in 3Q26, accelerating from 20% in 1H26, while overseas service revenue grew 60.9% YoY in 9M26. We believe the expanding installed base and increasing contribution from high-margin service contracts should support recurring revenue growth and gradually improve the company's revenue mix.

Outlook: Adopting more conservative assumptions; maintain Buy.

Management remains confident in achieving over 20% revenue growth and positive earnings growth in FY26, supported by a strong order backlog and continued commercialization of high-end products. While we remain constructive on UIH's long-term competitiveness, we plan to lower our 2026–27E revenue and earnings forecasts to reflect a more prudent outlook. Domestically, we see potential downside risks from the continued expansion of medical equipment VBP, which could exert greater pricing pressure over time, even though its impact has remained limited so far. In addition, we are cautious about the sustainability of hospital CAPEX following the potential withdrawal or normalization of government-backed equipment renewal and trade-in programs. Internationally, escalating trade tensions, procurement restrictions and geopolitical uncertainties could weigh on order conversion and delivery schedules. On the earnings side, sustained R&D investment, overseas expansion costs and potential further FX losses may continue to constrain near-term margin recovery. Nevertheless, we believe UIH's domestic market share gains, expanding high-end product portfolio, strong overseas growth potential and increasing contribution from high-margin services underpin its long-term growth prospects. We therefore maintain our Buy rating, while adopting more conservative near-term forecasts to better reflect potential execution risks.
