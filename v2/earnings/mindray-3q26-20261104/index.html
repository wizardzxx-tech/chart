3Q26 results: FX headwinds weighed on reported earnings growth.

Mindray reported revenue of RMB9.35bn in 3Q26, up 2.9% YoY, while net profit declined 2.5% YoY to RMB2.52bn. FX fluctuations remained a significant drag on reported performance. On a constant-currency basis, revenue grew 6.0% YoY, 3.1ppt above reported growth. Excluding FX gains and losses, net profit increased 1.8% YoY, suggesting that underlying operating performance was stronger than headline results indicated. Meanwhile, operating cash flow increased 2.1% YoY to RMB3.42bn, reflecting disciplined working capital management and solid earnings quality.

Revenue breakdown: Sequential domestic IVD recovery and emerging businesses offset equipment weakness.

By geography, domestic revenue declined 4% YoY to RMB4.46bn in 3Q26, while international revenue grew 11% YoY to RMB4.91bn, accounting for approximately 53% of total revenue. By segment, domestic IVD revenue declined 5% YoY to RMB2.36bn, but rose 4% QoQ, including 7% QoQ growth in immunoassay, supported by continued market share gains through key account penetration and laboratory automation installations despite ongoing pressure on testing volumes and prices. International IVD revenue maintained strong growth of 18% YoY to RMB1.37bn. Emerging businesses continued to outperform, with domestic APT Medical revenue growing 27% YoY to RMB832mn and overseas emerging businesses growing 44% YoY to RMB687mn. In contrast, domestic PMLS and medical imaging revenue declined 17% and 23% YoY to RMB651mn and RMB469mn, respectively, amid weak domestic equipment demand and continued channel destocking. Overseas PMLS and medical imaging revenue grew 2% and 9% YoY to RMB1.86bn and RMB996mn, respectively. According to management, channel inventories declined by approximately RMB500mn in 3Q26, reaching RMB4.3bn at quarter-end, of which roughly half was attributable to non-IVD products. Equipment inventories have already fallen to relatively low levels, and management expects destocking to be largely completed after a further reduction of several hundred million RMB in 4Q26.

Domestic outlook: Easing IVD policy pressure and completion of destocking to support growth recovery.

Management expects domestic revenue to return to positive growth for FY26 and accelerate further in 2027, supported by the completion of channel destocking and continued expansion in IVD and emerging businesses. On the policy front, management highlighted that recent adjustments to laboratory testing service fees across provinces have been more favorable than expected, with some testing fees remaining unchanged or even increasing. Management expects the impact on IVD ex-factory prices to remain below 10% in 2027, and believes that pricing pressure from IVD VBP is nearing an end following cumulative end-market price reductions of approximately 40% over the past six years. Meanwhile, refinements under DRG 3.0 could help stabilize testing volumes. The company targets over 10% domestic IVD revenue growth in 2027, driven by continued import substitution and penetration into high-end hospitals. Management expects its market share in biochemistry, immunoassay and coagulation to increase from approximately 15% in 2026 to 20% in 2027. The company also targets over 150 regional laboratory installations in 2027, compared with approximately 60 in 2026, while the conversion of its MT8000 installation pipeline into reagent sales should provide additional growth. For equipment, management expects domestic sales to recover as channel inventories normalize, although potential equipment VBP could create additional pricing pressure. Emerging businesses are expected to maintain over 20% growth in 2027.

International outlook: Broad-based growth momentum, although profitability headwinds persist.

Management expects international revenue growth to accelerate in 2027, with approximately 15% constant-currency growth considered a reasonable assumption. In 3Q26, Russia represented the primary disruption, with delays in product registration amendments resulting in an estimated US$40mn revenue shortfall. Excluding this impact, management estimated international revenue growth would have exceeded 15% YoY in USD terms. The disruption is considered temporary, with ultrasound shipments already partially resumed. Europe has maintained strong growth since 2024, supported by high market entry barriers, a stable competitive landscape and demand for automation solutions. North America is expected to accelerate in 2027, supported by a major US integrated healthcare network contract with potential annual purchases of US$40–50mn, driving regional growth of 10–15%. Latin America, India and the Middle East also maintained healthy growth momentum. On profitability, management expects an increasing reagent revenue mix and ongoing cost reductions to support further IVD gross margin improvement, following an approximately 1ppt increase in 2026 despite double-digit ex-factory price declines. However, rising semiconductor and electronic component prices could result in an additional RMB300–400mn cost burden in 2027, potentially reducing group gross margin by approximately 1ppt. FX volatility also remains a key uncertainty, following an estimated negative earnings impact of over RMB1bn in 2026. We believe the completion of domestic destocking, continued IVD market share gains and solid international growth should underpin a revenue recovery in 2027, although earnings growth could lag revenue improvement due to cost inflation and FX headwinds.

Mindray's 3Q26 results showed improving underlying business momentum despite continued FX headwinds and weakness in domestic equipment sales. Domestic IVD revenue recorded sequential recovery, supported by market share gains in high-end hospitals, while emerging businesses maintained strong growth. Looking ahead, management expects revenue growth to accelerate in 2027, driven by the completion of domestic channel destocking, easing IVD pricing pressure and continued import substitution. International growth is expected to remain robust, supported by sustained momentum in Europe, new customer contracts in North America and the normalization of Russian operations. However, earnings recovery may lag revenue growth, as RMB appreciation and rising semiconductor costs could offset margin improvements from a higher reagent revenue mix and operational efficiencies. We remain constructive on Mindray's revenue recovery outlook, while acknowledging near-term uncertainties surrounding profitability.
