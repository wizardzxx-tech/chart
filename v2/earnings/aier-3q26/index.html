Aier Eye Hospital

3Q26 Results & Outlook爱尔眼科

3Q26 revenue growth accelerated despite earnings pressure from tax-related charges.

Aier Eye Hospital reported revenue of Rmb18.34bn in 9M26, up 4.9% YoY, while attributable net profit declined 26.2% YoY to Rmb2.30bn and recurring net profit fell 20.3% YoY to Rmb2.49bn. In 3Q26, revenue growth accelerated to 7.1% YoY, reaching Rmb6.40bn, while attributable net profit declined 11.6% YoY to Rmb941mn. The earnings weakness was largely attributable to a one-off tax-related charge of approximately Rmb756mn in 1H26 following the company's self-assessment and supplementary corporate income tax payment. Excluding this impact, 9M26 attributable net profit would have declined by only c.2% YoY. Earnings were also affected by goodwill impairment and fair value changes in financial assets. According to the company, excluding these factors, 3Q26 earnings would have increased by nearly 7% YoY, suggesting that underlying operations remained relatively resilient despite a challenging industry environment.

Brazilian acquisition marks a major milestone in international expansion, albeit with near-term financial pressure.

Aier completed its acquisition of a leading Brazilian ophthalmology group, with the acquired business consolidated into its financial statements from August 1, 2026. This represents an important milestone in the company's international expansion, extending its geographical footprint from Europe, Southeast Asia and North America into South America. As of end-September, Aier operated 260 overseas facilities. We believe the acquisition should further strengthen Aier's global presence and provide a platform for future international growth. Nevertheless, overseas expansion has also weighed on the company's near-term financial performance. Financial expenses increased 88.9% YoY in 9M26, primarily due to acquisitions and FX fluctuations, while the company also recognized goodwill impairment losses during the period. Although the standalone earnings contribution from the Brazilian business was not disclosed, we expect its integration and operational optimization to take time, potentially creating some near-term pressure on group profitability.

Resilient domestic operations and international expansion underpin long-term growth opportunities.

We view Aier's positive revenue growth, particularly the acceleration in 3Q26, as an encouraging achievement against the backdrop of subdued consumer spending and continued pressure on China's ophthalmology services market. Looking ahead, we expect organic growth to become the primary driver of Aier's domestic business, with acquisitions gradually playing a less significant role in its overall expansion. Meanwhile, continued upgrades in refractive surgery technologies, increasing penetration of functional optical products, improvements in the cataract surgery mix, and the development of emerging services such as presbyopia management should support further growth through service upgrades and a more favorable business mix. Beyond China, we see substantial long-term opportunities for Aier to replicate its established hospital management capabilities, standardized operating procedures and high-quality medical services in international markets. While overseas expansion may create near-term integration costs and earnings volatility, we remain constructive on the company's long-term international growth potential as it leverages its proven operating model across new markets.

Aier Eye Hospital delivered resilient revenue growth in 3Q26 despite subdued consumer spending, although reported earnings remained under pressure from one-off tax-related charges and other non-operating factors. We believe the company's domestic growth will increasingly be driven by organic expansion and service upgrades, rather than acquisitions, supported by continued improvements in its refractive, optometry and cataract businesses. Meanwhile, the completion of its Brazilian acquisition marks a major milestone in its international expansion. While overseas integration may weigh on near-term profitability, we remain constructive on Aier's long-term growth potential, particularly its ability to replicate its established hospital management model and high-quality medical services in international markets.
