Snibe

3Q26 & 2027 Outlook新产业

Domestic business remains in a recovery phase, with reagent sales outperforming the broader industry.

Snibe's domestic revenue declined c.3% YoY in 3Q26, with instrument revenue down 18% and reagent revenue up c.1%, primarily due to a relatively high base in the prior year. Despite a c.7% decline in reagent ASP following the implementation of the second round of VBP, the company achieved c.8% growth in testing volume. Management believes 2025 marked the trough for its domestic business, with 2026 representing a transitional year of recovery. While overall testing demand remains weak, average testing volume per instrument at tertiary hospitals declined 3–4% YoY in 3Q26, a notable improvement from a decline of over 10% a year ago. Management estimates Snibe's domestic immunoassay market share at c.10%, up over 1ppt YoY, suggesting continued share gains and import substitution despite industry headwinds.

More favorable-than-expected testing service pricing reforms could ease pricing pressure.

Management believes the recently announced provincial testing service pricing proposals are more favorable than both market expectations and its previous assumptions. Domestic reagent prices have declined by c.30% cumulatively during 2024–26, with approximately two-thirds of testing items having already undergone major price adjustments and expected to maintain their current pricing levels. The remaining one-third may still face pricing pressure, although distributors could absorb part of the reductions given their relatively high margins. Management now expects overall reagent ASP to decline by only 3–5% in 2027, compared with its previous expectation of a 5–10% decline. Meanwhile, although DRG 3.0 remains focused on healthcare cost containment, improved reimbursement for complex diseases could benefit leading tertiary hospitals, which account for c.53% of Snibe's reagent revenue. Management also noted that the recent anti-corruption campaign has had no meaningful impact on its operations. Nevertheless, reimbursement controls and restrictions on testing remain key constraints on industry demand, and management expects overall industry testing volume to remain broadly flat in 2027.

Overseas growth remains resilient despite geopolitical disruptions in the Middle East and FX headwinds.

Overseas revenue increased c.12% YoY in 3Q26, below management's previous expectations, primarily due to geopolitical disruptions in the Middle East and RMB appreciation. Historically, the Middle East accounted for c.15% of overseas revenue, but ongoing regional conflicts and disruptions to Red Sea shipping routes have affected instrument deliveries, distributor expansion and procurement by local public hospitals. Demand for non-essential testing, including routine health check-ups, has also weakened. In contrast, other overseas markets continued to deliver solid growth, with revenue increasing 29% YoY in Europe, which now accounts for nearly 29% of overseas revenue, and 20% and 18% in Latin America and Africa, respectively. Management expects geopolitical and FX headwinds to persist into 4Q26, potentially limiting a near-term recovery in overseas growth, although it continues to target overseas revenue growth of over 15% for FY26.

Looking ahead, improving domestic fundamentals and a favorable revenue mix should support faster earnings growth in 2027.

With VBP-related pricing pressure gradually easing, management targets c.15% growth in domestic testing volume in 2027, which, together with an expected 3–5% decline in ASP, could support reagent revenue growth approaching 10%. Instrument sales will remain focused on high-end analyzers and laboratory automation systems, with overall instrument revenue expected to grow at a low-to-mid-single-digit rate. On profitability, the company continues to benefit from a higher contribution from high-margin reagents, overseas expansion and cost advantages from its in-house production of key raw materials. Snibe recorded over Rmb90mn in FX losses in 9M26, and management estimates that underlying earnings growth exceeded 20% after excluding FX effects. A moderation in RMB appreciation could further support reported earnings growth in 2027 through lower FX losses. We believe the gradual easing of domestic policy headwinds and continued overseas expansion should support a recovery in earnings growth, although the pace of domestic testing demand recovery and normalization of Middle East operations remain key uncertainties.

We hosted SNIBE's management with investors at our healthcare corporate access event. Management highlighted a gradual recovery in domestic operations, supported by continued market share gains and easing pricing pressure following better-than-expected provincial testing service pricing reforms. The company now expects domestic reagent ASP to decline by 3–5% in 2027, compared with its previous expectation of 5–10%, and targets c.15% testing volume growth. Overseas expansion remains solid in Europe, Latin America and Africa, although geopolitical disruptions in the Middle East and FX headwinds continue to weigh on near-term growth. Looking ahead, management expects improving domestic fundamentals, a favorable revenue mix and continued cost optimization to support stronger earnings growth in 2027, potentially outpacing revenue growth.
