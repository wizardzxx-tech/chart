3Q26 results: FX headwinds weighed on reported earnings growth.

Mindray reported revenue of RMB27.10bn in 9M26, up 4.9% YoY, while net profit declined 3.2% YoY to RMB7.56bn. In 3Q26, revenue increased 2.9% YoY to RMB9.35bn, while net profit declined 2.5% YoY to RMB2.52bn. FX fluctuations remained a significant drag on reported performance. On a constant-currency basis, revenue grew 7.4% YoY in 9M26 and 6.0% YoY in 3Q26, respectively 2.5ppt and 3.1ppt above reported growth. Excluding FX gains and losses, net profit increased 6.3% YoY in 9M26 and 1.8% YoY in 3Q26, suggesting that underlying operating performance was stronger than headline results indicated. Meanwhile, operating cash flow grew 14.1% YoY to RMB8.30bn in 9M26, reflecting disciplined working capital management and solid earnings quality.

Revenue breakdown: Domestic IVD recovery and emerging businesses offset equipment weakness.

By geography, domestic revenue declined 1.3% YoY to RMB12.73bn in 9M26, while international revenue grew 11.0% YoY to RMB14.36bn (16.0% in USD terms), accounting for 53% of total revenue. By segment, IVD revenue increased 7.8% YoY to RMB10.59bn, driven by 20% growth internationally and 2% growth domestically. Domestic IVD revenue rose 4% QoQ in 3Q26, including 7% QoQ growth in immunoassay, supported by continued market share gains through key account penetration and laboratory automation installations despite ongoing pressure on testing volumes and prices. Mindray secured nearly 400 new domestic TLA orders and completed nearly 300 installations in 9M26. Emerging businesses grew 20.5% YoY to RMB4.74bn, accelerating to 23% YoY in 3Q26, with both minimally invasive surgery and intervention growing more than 20%. In contrast, PMLS and MIS revenue declined 1.3% and 6.2% YoY to RMB7.33bn and RMB4.30bn, respectively, amid weak domestic equipment demand and continued channel destocking. According to management, channel inventories declined by approximately RMB500mn in 3Q26, reaching RMB4.3bn at quarter-end, of which roughly half was attributable to non-IVD products. Equipment inventories have already fallen to relatively low levels, and management expects destocking to be largely completed after a further reduction of several hundred million RMB in 4Q26.

Outlook: Destocking completion and IVD market share gains to drive growth acceleration in 2027.

Management expects domestic revenue to return to positive growth for FY26 and accelerate further in 2027, supported by the completion of channel destocking and continued expansion in IVD and emerging businesses. The company targets over 10% domestic IVD growth and over 20% growth in emerging businesses in 2027. Domestic IVD growth should be driven primarily by further penetration of large hospital accounts, conversion of the MT8000 installation pipeline into reagent sales, and expansion of regional laboratory networks. Management targets over 150 regional laboratory installations in 2027, compared with approximately 60 in 2026. Internationally, growth momentum remains strong in Europe, Latin America and Asia Pacific. A major US integrated healthcare network is expected to begin procurement in 2027, with potential annual purchases of approximately US$50mn, supporting faster North American growth. Management considers approximately 15% constant-currency international revenue growth a reasonable assumption for 2027. On profitability, while an increasing reagent revenue mix and internal cost reductions should support further IVD gross margin improvement, rising prices of memory chips and other electronic components could pressure margins in PMLS and MIS. We believe the completion of domestic destocking, continued IVD market share gains and strong emerging business growth should underpin a revenue recovery in 2027, although laboratory testing fee reforms, raw material inflation and FX volatility remain key risks to profitability.
