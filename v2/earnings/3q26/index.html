Snibe

新产业

3Q26 revenue growth moderated, with overseas business remaining the key growth driver.

Snibe reported revenue of RMB1.28bn in 3Q26, up 3.0% YoY, bringing 9M26 revenue to RMB3.70bn (+8.0% YoY). By geography, domestic revenue declined approximately 3.6% YoY in 3Q26, compared with 17.7% growth in 2Q26, while overseas revenue increased approximately 12.4% YoY, moderating from 27.4% growth in the previous quarter. For 9M26, domestic and overseas revenue grew 3.4% and 14.2% YoY, respectively. Despite the moderation in quarterly revenue growth, attributable net profit increased 20.6% YoY in 3Q26 to RMB523mn, outperforming topline growth.

Domestic business poised to stabilize, while overseas expansion offers substantial long-term growth potential.

We remain constructive on Snibe’s growth outlook. In China, the recently released draft adjustments to medical laboratory testing fees in Anhui and Jiangxi suggest relatively moderate pricing pressure on mainstream CLIA tests, including tumor markers, thyroid function and sex hormones, alleviating concerns over another round of significant price cuts. Together with the gradual digestion of previous VBP and DRG/DIP headwinds, as well as Snibe’s continued penetration into large hospitals, we expect its domestic business to gradually stabilize and return to growth. Overseas, we see substantial untapped opportunities, supported by the company’s expanding global footprint, increasing penetration into medium-to-large hospitals and growing installed base of high-end analyzers. In particular, the rising contribution from recurring reagent sales should underpin sustainable revenue growth and further improve the overseas revenue mix over time.

Gan & Lee

甘李药业

Stable domestic business with accelerating overseas expansion.

Gan & Lee reported 3Q26 revenue of Rmb1.15bn, up 16.9% YoY. For 9M26, domestic revenue grew 4.0% YoY to Rmb2.80bn, supported by higher insulin sales volumes, while international revenue increased 60.4% YoY to Rmb572mn. Overseas product sales rose 34.4% YoY, driven partly by continued shipments under the Brazil PDP program, while the company further expanded its international footprint through product registrations and licensing partnerships across Latin America, India, South Korea and Europe. Notably, Gan & Lee received a EUR62mn upfront payment in October under its European licensing agreement for bofanglutide, which could provide a meaningful earnings boost in 4Q26, subject to revenue recognition.

Positive clinical results pave the way for commercialization of innovative pipelines.

Gan & Lee has achieved encouraging clinical milestones for its two flagship innovative assets. Its biweekly GLP-1RA bofanglutide (GZR18) demonstrated up to 18.5% weight reduction over 52 weeks in its pivotal Phase III obesity trial, with its NDA accepted by China’s NMPA in September 2026. Meanwhile, its once-weekly insulin ludefen (GZR4) demonstrated superior HbA1c reduction versus once-daily basal insulins in three Phase III trials (SUPER-1/2/3), while the recent SUPER-8 trial established non-inferiority versus once-weekly insulin icodec without requiring an initial loading dose. We believe these positive results have substantially de-risked both programs, positioning bofanglutide for regulatory approval and GZR4 for an upcoming regulatory submission, potentially opening up new growth opportunities beyond the company’s existing insulin franchise.
