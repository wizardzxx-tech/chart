Gan & Lee Pharmaceuticals

Business & Pipeline Update

Business update and outlook: Domestic recovery and overseas expansion to support future growth.

Gan & Lee Pharmaceuticals reported revenue of RMB3.37bn in 9M26, up 11% YoY, and attributable net profit of RMB860mn, up approximately 5% YoY. In 3Q26, attributable net profit increased 16% YoY to RMB250mn. Total insulin sales volume grew 24% YoY to 90.6mn units in 9M26. Domestic revenue increased 4% YoY to RMB2.8bn, with business momentum gradually recovering from temporary disruptions in 1Q26. International revenue, including product sales and licensing-related income, grew 60% YoY to RMB570mn, primarily driven by strong growth in Brazil and other emerging markets. Looking ahead, we expect overseas expansion to become an increasingly important growth driver. The company's three insulin products, glargine, lispro and aspart, have received European approval, with shipments to its partner Sandoz commencing in September, while the Brazil PDP project continues to progress. On the manufacturing side, the Shandong facility is expected to expand domestic and overseas supply in 2027. Although capacity may remain tight in 1H27, management expects supply constraints to ease in 2H27. Together with licensing income from innovative drugs, management remains confident in achieving its 2026 earnings incentive target.

GLP-1 pipeline: Differentiated biweekly dosing and progress in global licensing.

Gan & Lee's lead GLP-1 candidate, bofanglutide (GZR18), is a biweekly injectable therapy whose NDA for obesity was submitted in China in September 2026. Management expects potential approval in late 2027 or early 2028, with the diabetes indication likely to follow approximately one quarter later. Management believes that biweekly dosing could improve patient convenience and treatment adherence by reducing injection frequency and potentially improving tolerability during the second week of treatment. In addition, an extension study has explored a once-monthly maintenance regimen, which could further strengthen the product's differentiation in long-term weight management, although this regimen is not expected to be included in the initial label. For commercialization, the company plans to leverage its established hospital-based diabetes sales network while strengthening digital marketing and innovative drug promotion capabilities, positioning GZR18 around convenience and cost-effectiveness in an increasingly competitive obesity market. Internationally, GZR18 has secured licensing agreements covering Latin America, India, South Korea and Europe. The EUR62mn upfront payment from its European partnership was received in October and is expected to be recognized as revenue within 2026. Beyond GZR18, the company's once-weekly oral peptide candidate GZR8072 has completed dosing in its Phase I study, while additional long-acting microsphere formulations and multi-target obesity therapies remain in early-stage development.

Once-weekly insulin: Positive Phase III results support upcoming NDA filing and global development.

Gan & Lee's fourth-generation insulin candidate, GZR4, has completed its Phase III clinical program in China, with an NDA submission planned for 4Q26 and the initiation of global multicenter Phase III trials, including in the US, targeted for late 2026 or early 2027. The company has completed four major clinical studies, SUPER-1, SUPER-2, SUPER-3 and SUPER-8, covering insulin-naive, previously treated and intensified-treatment patients. Notably, the SUPER-8 study, the first head-to-head trial comparing GZR4 with Novo Nordisk's once-weekly insulin icodec, met its primary endpoint of non-inferiority in glycemic control. Management believes GZR4's differentiated molecular design enables a faster onset of action and more responsive glucose control, while simplifying the transition from once-daily basal insulin without requiring additional loading-dose adjustments. These features could help address the relatively slow onset and complex dose-conversion requirements associated with existing once-weekly insulin products. As once-weekly basal insulin gains traction in China, we see meaningful room for further market penetration. Supported by its clinical evidence and Gan & Lee's established insulin sales infrastructure, GZR4 could become another important growth driver for the company over the medium to long term.

We hosted Gan & Lee's management with investors at our healthcare corporate access event. Management highlighted the gradual recovery in domestic insulin sales following temporary disruptions in 1Q26, alongside strong overseas growth driven by Brazil and the upcoming commercialization of three insulin products in Europe. Looking ahead, we see the company's innovative pipeline as a key driver of its long-term growth. Its biweekly GLP-1 candidate GZR18 has submitted its obesity NDA in China and secured licensing agreements across multiple overseas markets, including a EUR62mn upfront payment from its European partner. Meanwhile, its once-weekly insulin candidate GZR4 has delivered positive Phase III results, including non-inferiority versus insulin icodec, with an NDA filing expected in 4Q26. We believe continued international expansion and the commercialization of innovative therapies could support Gan & Lee's transition from a traditional insulin manufacturer into a broader metabolic disease player.
