LBX

Industry & Company Update

IndustryOperationsOutlookSummary

Industry overview: Industry consolidation and rising market concentration.

China's retail pharmacy industry is transitioning from store network expansion to consolidation, with store closures and market share gains by leading chains becoming key industry trends. The total number of pharmacies nationwide has declined from a peak of approximately 700,000 to slightly over 600,000, as smaller chains and independent pharmacies face increasing operating pressure. Meanwhile, tighter regulatory oversight, particularly through data-driven medical insurance supervision, has raised compliance requirements, while limited access to outpatient reimbursement qualifications has further strengthened the competitive advantages of leading pharmacy chains. On the policy front, the government has encouraged industry consolidation and M&A activities, suggesting further room for market concentration to increase. In addition, innovative and originator drug manufacturers are increasingly expanding their presence in the out-of-hospital market, potentially enhancing the role of retail pharmacies in prescription drug distribution, patient services and chronic disease management.

Company operations: Improving SSSG driven by stronger customer traffic.

LBX currently operates approximately 15,000 stores across nearly 20 provinces, with the majority located in lower-tier cities. SSSG has improved sequentially throughout 2026, reaching mid-single-digit growth on average in the first nine months, while retail sales increased at a high-single-digit pace. Growth was primarily driven by stronger customer traffic, with average spending per customer remaining broadly stable, reflecting the company's ability to capture market share amid ongoing industry consolidation. Stores qualified for outpatient medical insurance reimbursement delivered particularly strong performance. Meanwhile, LBX has continued to implement operational reforms, including reorganizing its procurement structure around therapeutic areas, streamlining SKUs, increasing the contribution from private-label products, and improving procurement and promotional efficiency. The company has also strengthened customer engagement through dedicated originator drug sections, free health screenings and chronic disease management services. These initiatives have started to support operating efficiency and profitability in the core offline retail business, although faster growth in relatively lower-margin DTP and O2O businesses continues to partly offset improvements in the reported gross margin.

Company outlook: Sustainable organic growth and margin improvement.

Looking ahead, management expects SSSG to remain resilient, supported by ongoing industry consolidation, advantages in outpatient reimbursement qualifications and continued operational improvements, although seasonal fluctuations in influenza incidence may create short-term volatility in the comparison base. Management also anticipates further gross margin improvement in the core retail business, driven by product mix optimization, procurement efficiency gains and more refined store operations. Together with positive operating leverage from mature stores, these initiatives could support faster profit growth relative to revenue growth. Regarding network expansion, LBX plans to prioritize increasing store density and operating efficiency in existing markets rather than entering new provinces, leveraging a combination of selective new store openings, small-scale acquisitions, franchising and pharmacy alliances. The company currently has an alliance network of nearly 20,000 pharmacies, which could provide additional growth opportunities through deeper supply chain integration, managed-store operations and selective acquisitions. In addition, LBX is rolling out AI-driven initiatives covering store personnel, merchandise and store operations. Encouraging initial pilot results suggest that broader implementation could provide an additional driver of SSSG and operating efficiency improvements in the coming years.

We hosted LBX's management with investors at our recent investor conference. Management highlighted improving same-store sales growth, driven primarily by stronger customer traffic as industry consolidation accelerates and leading pharmacy chains gain market share. The company expects this momentum to remain sustainable, supported by its advantages in outpatient medical insurance reimbursement qualifications and ongoing operational reforms. Meanwhile, product mix optimization, procurement efficiency improvements and positive operating leverage should support further margin expansion and earnings growth. Looking ahead, LBX plans to focus on organic growth and network optimization within existing markets, while leveraging its extensive pharmacy alliance network and AI-driven operational initiatives to unlock additional growth opportunities.
