Where the bench fits

A lab can buy synthetic fragments, assemble a construct, clone and grow it, purify plasmid DNA and confirm its sequence. Buying a finished, sequence-verified plasmid can replace several of these activities. Fluorometry and a gel check concentration and size. Sequence data confirm the intended construct when coverage and read quality are adequate.

The screen represents sequence review. A sequencing service may perform the measurement off-site.
