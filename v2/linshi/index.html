Dashenlin: Corporate Day takeaways — Structural consolidation and improving operating efficiency

Accelerating store expansion and improving operating efficiency. Dashenlin has accelerated its network expansion, particularly in 2H26, targeting 500-600 self-built stores, 500-600 stores through M&A and over 2,000 franchised stores in 2026, above its initial plan. SSSG has been primarily driven by higher customer traffic, with average transaction value broadly stable. Prescription drugs and OTC products, accounting for 40% and 38% of sales, respectively, remain the key growth drivers. O2O sales account for c.12% of revenue, with a gross margin of over 30% and a net margin comparable to offline operations at c.5%. Management attributed the improvement in net margin to gross margin expansion, a higher contribution from franchised stores, lower rental costs, improved labor productivity and higher sales per store.

Rising entry barriers to drive structural industry consolidation. Management believes that increasingly stringent requirements for medical insurance accreditation have structurally raised entry barriers for the retail pharmacy industry. Expansion through M&A and franchising, rather than new store openings, should help contain incremental industry supply. Management expects 30-40k store closures in 2026 and believes industry consolidation could continue for several years, with the nationwide pharmacy count potentially declining from c.600k in 2025 to 500k over time, and the number of medical insurance-accredited pharmacies from 450k to 300k. Meanwhile, future medical insurance compliance inspections could increasingly focus on smaller pharmacies. Management sees potential for the combined market share of the top three pharmacy chains to double from c.15% currently to 30% over the next decade.

Faster SSSG and further margin expansion in 2027. Management expects a meaningful sequential improvement in 4Q26, albeit with a YoY decline given the high base associated with influenza-related demand in 4Q25. Looking into 2027, management expects more store additions and accelerating SSSG, supported by customer traffic gains from industry consolidation, incremental sales of non-drug products and broader adoption of innovative drugs. In particular, GLP-1 products currently contribute c.2% of revenue, with sales potentially doubling in 2027. Management expects further net margin expansion in 2027, albeit at a more moderate pace than in the past two years, driven by a higher franchise mix and continued efficiency gains at directly operated stores. With c.Rmb7bn in cash and lower interest expenses following the maturity of its convertible bonds in October, the company expects to maintain its dividend payout ratio at c.75% in 2026.
