AmoyDx: Competitive strengths remain intact, but the next growth engine is less visible

Domestic growth outlook. Unlike conventional IVD companies such as Mindray and SNIBE, AmoyDx operates in the oncology companion diagnostics market, with a distinct competitive landscape and commercialization model. The medical anti-corruption campaign has encouraged a shift from outsourced to in-hospital testing, favoring AmoyDx given its established hospital channels. However, I see increasing structural constraints on domestic market growth. Penetration of molecular testing in lung cancer, where companion diagnostics have historically played the most significant role, is already relatively high, while expansion into other cancer types has been slower. Emerging testing opportunities in breast cancer, for example, often involve lower-priced methodologies such as FISH and IHC. Moreover, the rapid development of innovative drugs, particularly ADCs and bispecific antibodies, does not necessarily translate into comparable growth in high-value molecular diagnostics, as many of these therapies either do not require companion diagnostics or rely on relatively low-cost testing methods. This limits the incremental growth contribution from higher-priced products such as large NGS panels and multiplex PCR assays. Potential pricing pressure from broader reimbursement coverage and eventual VBP remains another medium-term risk.

New growth opportunities. I see some potential benefits from Sinopharm's acquisition of control, particularly through broader distribution channels, penetration into lower-tier markets and potential international expansion in Belt and Road markets. However, given that oncology diagnosis and treatment remain concentrated in major hospitals in larger cities, I am not particularly optimistic about the incremental domestic growth opportunity from channel expansion alone. Internationally, management indicated that its overseas team restructuring has been completed, which could support a recovery following previously subdued performance. Nevertheless, I would need to see more tangible evidence of overseas commercial execution before incorporating meaningful growth acceleration into my base case.

Investment positioning and coverage considerations. I would position AmoyDx as an established niche market leader with relatively reasonable valuation but limited visibility on its next structural growth driver. While its competitive position remains solid, I see relatively limited scope for near-term earnings acceleration or meaningful re-rating. My impression is that investor interest remains subdued, reflecting both its relatively small market capitalization and modest growth prospects. Given the limited growth visibility, potential pricing risks and lack of identifiable catalysts, I think it is worth revisiting our current rating, including a potential downgrade. More broadly, given limited investor engagement, I would also suggest discussing whether AmoyDx should remain a priority within our coverage universe.
